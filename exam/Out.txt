  Part C · Spectral trapezoid vs a low-order adaptive rule   [1 pt]
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  On the circle z = e^{iθ} the Bessel integrand is periodic.  For a periodic
  integrand the trapezoid converges exponentially, so it should beat the
  book's open-4 rule here — that rule is low order, so this is a fair test of
  'right tool for the job', not a claim that adaptivity is bad in general.

  • Periodic trapezoid vs the (low-order) adaptive open-4 rule, both for J₃(5)
